How to Apply for National Identity Card in Nepal (Step-by-Step Guide)

February 28, 2026 6 min read Eksana Editorial Team
← Back to Blog

Nepal's National Identity Card (NID), officially known as Rastriya Parichaya Patra, has become an essential document for every Nepali citizen aged 16 and above. This mandatory identification card serves as your gateway to voting, banking services, government benefits, and numerous other official transactions across the country.

In 2026, the Department of National ID and Civil Registration (DoNIDCR) has significantly enhanced the application process by introducing digital features, faster NIN issuance through mobile apps, and reduced biometric enrollment wait times.

Who Can Apply?

You qualify for a National Identity Card if you meet these requirements:

  • You must be a Nepali citizen with a valid citizenship certificate.
  • You must be at least 16 years old at the time of application. There is no upper age limit.
  • Minors under 16 years can obtain provisional identity cards that can be upgraded to full NIDs once they reach the eligible age.
  • Non-Resident Nepalis (NRNs) must follow a separate application process through Nepali embassies and consulates abroad.

Step-by-Step Online Pre-Enrollment Process

The online pre-enrollment system typically takes just 10–15 minutes and significantly reduces your time at the enrollment center.

Step 1: Access the Official DoNIDCR Portal

Visit the official Department of National ID and Civil Registration website at enrollment.donidcr.gov.np or donidcr.gov.np. Look for the 'Login for Individuals' option on the homepage.

Step 2: Register and Verify Your Mobile Number

Enter your mobile number — this must be a number registered in your name. Click 'Send OTP' and enter the one-time password within the specified time limit to verify your number.

Step 3: Select 'New Enrollment'

Navigate to the 'New Enrollment' section and select your province, district, and preferred enrollment center from the dropdown menus.

Step 4: Fill in Personal and Family Details

Provide accurate information including personal details (full name in both English and Nepali, date of birth, gender, blood group, marital status), address details, family information (parents, spouse, grandparents), and citizenship certificate details.

Important: Double-check all entries before proceeding. Correcting information after submission can be extremely difficult and may require restarting the entire application process.

Step 5: Book Your Biometric Appointment

The system will display available appointment slots at your chosen enrollment center. In 2026, most urban centers offer appointments within one to two weeks. Popular centers in Kathmandu tend to fill up quickly, so booking at least a week in advance is advisable.

Step 6: Generate and Print Your Application Token

Once you've booked your appointment, the system generates a unique 16-digit application number. Download or print the application slip, which contains your appointment details and a QR code for quick verification at the enrollment center.

Pro Tip: Take screenshots of all confirmation pages and note down your 16-digit application number in multiple places — you'll need it frequently.

Visiting the Enrollment Center for Biometric Capture

Arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment. The staff will verify your documents and direct you to the biometric capture station where trained officials will capture:

  1. Facial Photograph — Follow the operator's instructions. Remove glasses, caps, or any accessories.
  2. Fingerprints — All ten fingerprints using a digital scanner. Ensure fingers are clean and dry.
  3. Iris Scan — Both eyes using specialized iris recognition technology.
  4. Digital Signature — Signed on an electronic signature pad.

The entire verification and capture process typically takes 10 to 20 minutes. You'll receive an acknowledgment slip confirming your biometric data has been successfully recorded.

How to Check Your Application Status and Obtain Your NIN

Your National Identification Number (NIN) is a unique 10-digit identifier generated once your biometric data is processed.

  • SMS: Send N [space] [16-digit application number] to 30001 to receive your NIN within seconds.
  • Online Portal: Log in to donidcr.gov.np and navigate to the 'Status Check' section.
  • Nagarik App: Download the official Nagarik app and link your NIN for real-time push notifications.
  • Automatic SMS: The DoNIDCR system automatically sends SMS updates at key milestones.

Common Issues and Troubleshooting

OTP Not Received During Login

Verify that your mobile number is properly registered in your name with your telecom provider. Network congestion can sometimes delay SMS delivery, so wait 2–3 minutes before requesting a new OTP.

No Appointment Slots Available

Try selecting alternative centers in nearby districts or check the portal during early morning hours (6–8 AM) when new slots are typically released. The system refreshes weekly.

Document Rejection at Enrollment Center

The most common cause is an expired or damaged citizenship certificate. Ensure your certificate is original, clearly legible, and issued by authorized government authorities. Photocopies or laminated documents without originals will be rejected.

Delays Beyond Expected Timeline

The 2026 nationwide rollout is being implemented in phases, with priority given to Phase 1 districts. Monitor the official DoNIDCR website for district-wise updates and revised timelines.

Application Fees and Charges

The first-time NID issuance is completely free of charge. However, nominal charges apply for: card renewal (approximately NPR 500 after the 10-year validity period) and card replacement (approximately NPR 1,000 for lost, stolen, or damaged cards — a police FIR is required before applying for replacement).

What to Do After Receiving Your NID

  • Collect in Person: NID cards must be collected personally by the applicant. Proxy collection is not permitted. Your card is valid for 10 years.
  • Link Your NIN: Use your 10-digit NIN to register for voter lists, link bank accounts, update passport records, and access government services.
  • Integrate with Nagarik App: Download the Nagarik application and link your NIN for digital access to your identity card.
  • Safe Storage: Keep your physical NID card in a secure place. Consider keeping a laminated photocopy for daily use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for NID without a citizenship certificate?

No, a valid Nepali citizenship certificate is absolutely mandatory for NID application. Without citizenship documentation, you cannot proceed with the enrollment process.

Is the National ID card mandatory for voting?

Yes, the NID is directly linked to voter registration in Nepal. Your NIN automatically enrolls you in the electoral database, making it essential for exercising your voting rights.

Is digital NID available in 2026?

Yes. Electronic National ID (e-NID) is being piloted in select districts through the Nagarik mobile application. The program is expanding throughout 2026, with plans for nationwide digital ID access by 2027.

How long is the NID valid?

Your National Identity Card is valid for 10 years from the date of issuance. You will need to renew it after the expiry date to maintain its validity for official purposes.
